The Board and its devices

The Board is everyone seated On stage in a hall's Main room: presenters and board members. An On stage seat reads and writes messages and nothing else. It shares no file, drives no screen and sends nothing to the show network, and the admin seated On stage is held to the same. Its device is never given the hall's code, and keeps no copy of a file: a Board member reads a file with Look, and downloads one only when the backstage team shows it with Download ticked.

AGMessage reads no device's name: each page lays out by the screen's size and tests what the browser can do.

Seating the Board

There are three ways to an On stage seat, and a code is not one of them. Without the On stage invite link, the sign-in page does not offer the seat: it says On stage seats come with an invite link from the backstage team.

With the On stage invite link

  1. On the admin page, open the hall's ⋯ → Roles & invites, and copy the On stage link (📋 Copy). Copied at the laptop with the token, it says localhost: put the LAN address in its place before sending it.
  2. Send it to each presenter and board member, or open it on each Board tablet.
  3. The link opens the sign-in page with the code filled in and On stage chosen: they type their name and tap Join.

Once joined, the hall page takes the code and the link out of its own address. The browser's history still holds the sign-in page the link opened, so after a shared device was used, make a new link with 🔄 New On stage link. A reload, a dropped Wi-Fi or a restart of AGMessage keeps the seat.

With a Board account

  1. On the Accounts page, Add a person (or Edit… an account).
  2. Under Halls and rooms, tick On stage (Board member) beside the hall; the hall is ticked with it.
  3. They sign in on the Sign in tab, and the hall opens On stage.

To seat many accounts at once, signed in with an account that creates accounts: the hall's ⋯ on the admin page → Board members…, tick, Save. An account that only reads cannot sit On stage: Reads only: On stage needs Send messages.

By moving a guest's seat

Watching the hall on the admin page, change a guest's Seat to On stage. Their page reloads into the seat.

△ Watch for A browser signed in to an account cannot join with a code or an invite link. A tablet shared between an account and an invite must Sign out first.

The chat view

Each person's Chat view is By seat, Simple or Full. By seat, the default, is Simple where they sit On stage and Full everywhere else. It is set in three places: the Chat view column of People in this room on the admin page and the person's ⋯ in the admin's own hall page (by whoever the admin page lets in), and the Accounts page's editor (by whoever creates accounts). Nobody sets their own.

The simple view puts the chat first: one reading column, the response after the words it answers, and a ⋯ sheet in place of a menu. It starts dark until the person picks Light, with 22 px text on a tablet and 20 px on a phone. It acts on Tab, Enter and Escape (and ← → on Shown to you's grip), and opens no menu of its own.

What a Board member does

  • Got it and Reply. Under the newest message from backstage that waits for them, and under each waiting 🔥 message: Got it (Got all N when several wait) and Reply, each a button. Got it tells the backstage team, by name. A tap on any other message brings its own row, with Pin for me.
  • Questions. A tap on an answer, or Answer in words…; once answered, Change brings the answers back.
  • Priority. 🔥 Priority before Send; it turns off after each send. A 🔥 message from backstage that scrolled out of view waits in a strip above the chat.
  • Help. Help, then what it is about (Sound, Vision, Lighting, Stage, Presenter, IT / network or Something else): the tap sends it at once. The chip then reads that it was asked, then who is on the way.
  • The ⋯ sheet. Text size, Clock or countdown, Theme, Full screen where the device has it, and Leave the hall….

Files on the Board's pages

A file the backstage team shares in the chat has Look under it, a filled button, and a tap on the file's line does the same. It opens in the person's own 📺 Shown to you, where they page, zoom and find in it. Nobody else is told.

  • A tablet held sideways (900 px wide or more): Shown to you is a column beside the chat. It opens wide while it holds a file, over half the screen. The bar between the chat and the column drags it wider or narrower (or Tab to it and use ← →), and the tablet keeps the width. Its ⇥ closes it to a strip on the right edge, "📺 Shown to you • 2 ⇤", and a tap on the strip brings it back. Nothing in it is lost until it is cleared.
  • A phone, or a tablet held upright: Look opens the 📺 Shown to you tab at the full width, and Chat goes back.
  • A document the backstage team sends to the side bar arrives as a card in Shown to you, with its own Look, which shows it over the chat at the full screen.

A file is closed with Close under it, and opened again with Look on its message.

The clock or countdown

Beside the hall's name the simple view shows the time of day ("🕐 14:12"), the hall's Stagewatch countdown ("⏱ 4:30 left", then "Wrap up", then "0:30 over"), or nothing. When the countdown is not live it reads --:-- with why.

  • The person chooses on their device: ⋯ → Clock or countdown (Time of day, Countdown or Off), kept on that device.
  • It can be chosen for them: Their choice (the clock at first), Time of day, Stagewatch countdown or Off, on the admin page's people list, the hall page's People, or the Accounts page.
  • Lock it on their devices keeps that choice on their devices; the sheet then says Set by the admin for you. Anyone who sets the choice may pick it, but only the admin locks it. For a person whose chat view is Full it reads Applies in the simple view.

What an On stage seat may do

Three switches for each person, each off until the admin turns it on, on the admin page's On stage column, the person's ⋯, or the Accounts page's When On stage:

  • May send to a countdown screen: their message can replace the line under the countdown on this hall's screens (160 characters at most).
  • May send to people On stage: their message can be shown beside the chat of everyone On stage.
  • Got it clears the message: a message they got folds to one line, with Show to bring it back.

With either of the first two on, an Also on row sits above their message box, a chip each, off after every send; the message then says where it went as each screen and page confirms.

What the backstage team shows them

Backstage, the Board chip in the hall page's header puts something on every Board page at once (Show Stagewatch, Show the clock, Show holding, Show blank, Show a web page…, Take down), and a file's Show the Board shows a picture of a page, or the document. On a person's ⋯, Show them does the same for one person.

  • A take covers the page, its bar saying what it is and who sent it, with Back to chat and Minimise (and Escape). Minimise puts it in Shown to you as a card, with Look.
  • A 🔥 message breaks through in a strip, with Open chat.
  • A take taken down goes under Earlier in Shown to you, where Look opens it again on that device alone.
  • A document shown with Download ticked has Download; nothing else On stage can be downloaded, and a long press on what is shown offers no save.
  • Screens & pages… → People's pages says what each page confirmed: a tick only once the take is on the screen in front of them.

Keeping a device awake

The simple view asks the device to keep its screen on. Where it cannot, the ⋯ sheet says one of three things:

  • This device may sleep: open AGMessage over HTTPS. Trust AGMessage's certificate once on the device, then open it with https://, same address and port.
  • This device may sleep: set its screen to stay on in its own settings (Auto-Lock or Screen timeout). The browser cannot keep it awake: set Auto-Lock (iPad) or Screen timeout (Android) to Never for the show.
  • This device would not stay awake (its battery may be low): plug it in.

Trusting the certificate on a tablet or phone

  1. Open /tls on AGMessage's address and check its fingerprint against the admin page's 🔒 Encrypted connections.
  2. Press Download the certificate.
  3. iPhone or iPad: open the download → Settings → Profile Downloaded → Install; then Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings, and turn it on. Android: Settings → Security → Encryption & credentials → Install a certificate → CA certificate, and choose the file (menus differ by maker).
  4. Open AGMessage with https://.

Full screen and text size

  • ⋯ → Full screen where the device has it; an iPhone has none, so hold it upright. A take offers Full screen on its bar too, and never goes full screen by itself.
  • ⋯ → Text size: Aa− and Aa+, from 70 % to 220 %. On an iPad mini, or with the tablet further away than an arm's length, press Aa+.

Screens on TVs and tablets

A screen is a browser holding a screen's link, made on the hall page's Screens & pages…: Setting up: screens. On the screen itself:

  • A double-click makes it full screen. Over HTTPS it keeps itself awake; otherwise set the device to stay on.
  • A small dot in the corner shows its link to AGMessage; ?status=hide on the link hides it. With no holding image it is black with its name, faint; ?idle=blank hides the name.
  • It comes back by itself after a dropped connection, and never shows the audience a "busy".
  • A PDF on a screen needs Safari 16.4 or Chrome 103; on an older browser share the page as a picture instead.
  • A link that is wrong or no longer in use says so: This screen link is not (or no longer) in use. Ask for the current link.

Phones backstage, and remote phones

  • The hall page fits a phone held upright, its header the same at every width; on a phone a menu opens as a sheet from the bottom, with ‹ Back for its submenus.
  • A message written while offline says it is sent when the connection is back, and is sent once.
  • Phones off the venue network join through the internet link: Setting up: the internet link. It has 150 places; a phone refused is told how long to wait, and comes back by itself.
  • Phones on the venue Wi-Fi reach the laptop only where Windows lets AGMessage in on that network: the firewall and networks.

What to check on a device

Before a show, on each kind of device the Board will use, served over HTTPS with the certificate trusted, and with a backstage page on a laptop beside it, upright and sideways:

  1. The screen stays lit for 10 minutes untouched, or the ⋯ sheet says why not.
  2. Help opens; a category sends it and closes; the chip says it was asked.
  3. A file's Look opens it readable; sideways, the column drags wider, ⇥ closes it and the strip brings it back.
  4. Got it, Reply and Answer in words… with the on-screen keyboard up: the reply line and Send stay in view.
  5. 🔥 Priority, and the strip for a 🔥 message out of view.
  6. The clock reads the hall's time; the countdown its words; --:-- when Stagewatch is offline.
  7. A picture, a quote and a document shown to them; Back to chat; Earlier and its Look.
  8. Lock the device for 2 minutes and wake it: the page is back with the new messages. Turn Wi-Fi off, send, turn it on: the message lands once.
  9. From their seat, the person reads what is new and what is urgent within 5 seconds, at the Text size they chose.

When something goes wrong

  • "Reconnecting…" above the chat after 2 seconds, and "Offline · reconnecting" after 5: the page comes back by itself, at once when the device wakes or is back online. What they wrote waits and is sent once.
  • An act refused with "Not connected — AGMessage is reconnecting." Do it again once the page is back.
  • "AGMessage now runs another build — reload this page when you can." The page could not reload by itself onto the new build: reload it.
  • To see what they see: on the admin page, the person's ⋯ → Open their view. Every control there is theirs; act from your own page.
  • The device may sleep: keeping a device awake.
  • A file too small: on a tablet held sideways, drag the column wider; on a phone or an upright tablet, the Shown to you tab gives it the whole width. Fit width and + in the file's own bar zoom it.
