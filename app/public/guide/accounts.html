People, accounts and rights

Who signs in, what each may do, and how to add, change and remove them. Crew and the Board may sign in with accounts; presenters and guests may join a hall with its code instead.

The Accounts page

The Accounts page (/users.html) has two tabs, People and Audit log. It is reached from the halls board's Accounts link, and from the admin page's 👥 Accounts and access: Manage people, roles and halls.

  • The People tab is for an account allowed to create accounts. An account that may only read the audit log sees the Audit log tab alone.
  • The admin token is not an account, so it cannot open the People tab. Make the first admin account with the token (Setting up: the first admin account), then sign in as that account.

The People list shows each person's Name (with the username, and "turned off" or "one-time password" where so), Role ("(adjusted)" when rights differ from the role's), Halls and rooms (for example "Hall 2 (Main, Legal) · On stage") and Last signed in. Each row has Edit… and a ⋯, also opened by a right-click.

Roles

A role is where a person's rights start. The editor ticks the role's rights, and any of them can be changed for that one person.

RoleWhich hallsWhat it starts with
AdminEvery hallEverything: accounts, rights and settings. Only an admin makes or changes an admin.
Project managerEvery hallMessages, files, screens, the Board's pages, Stagewatch, the show network; sets halls up and hands out their codes; reads the audit log.
Senior techEvery hallMessages, files, screens, the Board's pages, Stagewatch, the show network.
TechTheir hallsMessages, files, screens, the Board's pages, Stagewatch.
StaffTheir hallsMessages, files, and information for the Board's pages. A new person starts as Staff.
ViewerTheir hallsRead only.

△ Watch for Only the Admin role can create accounts, change settings and export the audit log. The sign-in page tells people to ask their project manager for an account, so give your project managers Create accounts and set their roles, halls and rights if they are to make them.

Rights

The editor's Rights come in three groups; a right that differs from the role's is marked "(added)" or "(removed)".

In the room

  • See a hall: its messages, files, countdown and screens
  • Send messages, replies, action prompts and help requests
  • Share files for download, or forward one into a hall

Show

  • Change what a hall's screens and people's pages show
  • Create, rename, move and revoke screens
  • Send to Stagewatch: the speaker's screen, or to the operator to pass on
  • Show files, pages and quotes to the people on stage
  • Show web pages on people's pages (the Board's or one person's)
  • Propose commands to Patterns and PlayMap (to the show desk)

Run AGMessage

  • Create halls and their rooms, set them up (names, Stagewatch link, holding image) and hand out their guest codes
  • Create accounts and set their roles, halls and rights
  • App settings: AI key, HTTPS, the tunnel, the show network
  • Read the audit log
  • Export the whole audit log, signed, to check on another computer (an account with it signs in through the tunnel only when admins may)

The rights of the first two groups count only in the halls the account is given (or every hall, for the roles that have them). A side room needs that room on the account, whatever its role, but an admin, who is in every room.

Add a person

  1. Open the Accounts page (the halls board's Accounts) and press Add a person.
  2. Fill in Name and Username: 2 to 32 lowercase letters, digits, dots, dashes or underscores, starting with a letter or digit. A username cannot be changed later.
  3. Choose the Role, and optionally the Chat view.
  4. Under Halls and rooms, tick each hall (its Main room and its rights there), any side rooms, and On stage (Board member) where they sit on the Board. Or tick Every hall's Main room, including new ones (not their other rooms).
  5. Change any of the Rights if this person differs from their role.
  6. Press Save. The One-time password shows once: give it to the person in person or by phone (Copy copies it), then press Done. It works once; they choose their own at their first sign-in.

A new account has no clock choice and no On stage switches. To set them, save the person, then Edit….

Change a person

  1. On the person's row press Edit… (or Enter on the row).
  2. Change what you need, then press Save. Only what changed is sent.
  • A change to their role, rights, halls or rooms ends their open pages, which reconnect with the new rights: Save says their open pages reconnect with the new rights.
  • A change to their chat view, clock or On stage switches reaches their open pages at once: Save says their open pages draw it now.
  • A side room can be given without its hall. Ticking On stage ticks the hall; unticking the hall unticks On stage.

Many accounts into one room

For an account that creates accounts (not the token), a hall's ⋯ on the admin page → Board members… ticks who sits On stage in its Main room; a side room's ⋯ → Members… ticks the accounts given that room. (Both are also on the halls board's Set up… → Rooms.) Tick or untick, then Save: each account is saved on its own, and their open pages reload. A row you may not change is greyed, with why.

Seats, and Board accounts

Seats are On stage, Backstage and Observer in a hall's Main room, and Member and Observer in a side room. An account's seat comes from its rights in that room:

  • On stage in a hall's Main room, where the hall is one of its On stage halls and it may send messages there.
  • Backstage wherever else it may send messages; an Observer where it may only read.

An account given On stage (Board member) always joins that Main room On stage, reads and writes messages there and nothing else, and is never told the hall's code. That holds for an admin account too. In its other rooms it keeps the seat its rights give it.

Passwords and sign-ins

  • A password is 10 to 128 characters, not a common one, not one character repeated, and contains neither the username nor the person's name.
  • At a first sign-in with a one-time password, the person chooses their own (Choose your password); nothing else works until they do.
  • Anyone changes their own from the halls board's Password: Current password, New password, Save password. Their other devices are signed out.
  • A wrong username, a wrong password and a turned-off account all read the same: That username and password don't match.
  • Five wrong passwords for a username within 15 minutes hold that username where they came from (a device's address, or the whole internet link as one place), and twenty from one address hold that address: Too many attempts — wait a few minutes and try again. A username is never held on the AGMessage laptop itself, though twenty wrong passwords from it hold it too.
  • A sign-in ends after 14 hours unused or 7 days old.

New one-time password, sign out, turn off, remove

Each is on the person's ⋯ on the Accounts page, and each asks first.

New one-time password…
Signs them out everywhere, stops the old password, and shows a new one-time password once. It also lifts a hold from wrong passwords. Only an admin resets an admin's password.
Sign out everywhere…
Closes every page they have open, on every device; they sign in again with their password.
Turn off… and Turn on
Signs them out everywhere and keeps them out until it is turned on again. Their messages stay.
Remove…
They can no longer sign in, and it cannot be undone. Their messages stay.

Nobody turns off or removes their own account, and the last active admin cannot be turned off, demoted or removed: make another admin first.

Who may change whom

  • Nobody changes their own role, rights, halls, rooms, chat view, clock or On stage switches: Nobody changes their own role, rights or halls — ask another admin.
  • Only an admin makes or changes an admin, and only an admin gives the rights to create accounts, change app settings or export the audit log.
  • An account holding any of those three is changed, reset, signed out, turned off or removed only by an admin.
  • Someone allowed to create accounts who is not an admin manages only accounts within their own rights, halls and rooms, and gives only what they hold themselves. The editor offers only what the server allows, and greys the rest.

Guests who join with a code

A guest joins on the sign-in page's Join with a code tab with the room's code and a name. What their seat allows:

  • Backstage, in a hall's Main room: messages, files, and sends to Stagewatch. In a locked hall, Backstage guests may also change what people's pages show.
  • On stage: messages only, and only with the On stage invite link (seating the Board).
  • Member, in a side room: messages and files. Observer: read only.

A guest cannot take the name of an account. On the admin page, a guest's seat is changed in its Seat column; an account's is changed on the Accounts page. Log out… on a person sends them out: a guest can join again with the code, and an account can open the hall again until the hall is taken off the account.

Admin rights and the internet link

An account allowed to create accounts, change app settings or export the audit log cannot sign in, or use those rights, through the internet link, unless Let accounts with admin rights sign in through the Cloudflare tunnel (off: admins sign in on the venue network only) is ticked under 👥 Accounts and access. Leave it off unless an admin must work from off-site.

Accounts held aside

When a laptop cannot vouch for the accounts file (a data folder copied from another laptop or Windows account, a profile rebuilt, an older copy put back), it holds the accounts aside. Account sign-ins are refused until they are opened; guests still join with codes. The sign-in page says Accounts are not open yet.

  1. At the AGMessage laptop, open the admin page and sign in with the token.
  2. Under 👥 Accounts and access, find 🔐 Accounts held aside. Each account is listed with its name, role and halls, and whether it is as this laptop last saved it, changed since, or new to it.
  3. Tick those to keep, press Adopt the ticked… and type the token. An account's ⋯ also offers With a one-time password, to adopt it with a new password.
  4. Give each one-time password shown to its person. Leave out the rest with Leave the rest out…, or begin with none with Start with no accounts….

An account that can manage AGMessage asks for a tick of its own: adopt it only if the folder has been in your hands since it was given that.

The audit log

Every sign-in, change of rights, hall, screen and send to another show system is a row in the audit log, each chained to the one before with this laptop's key, so a row edited, removed or reordered shows. It never holds a password, a token or a screen's link.

  • Read it on the Accounts page's Audit log tab, newest first, with Check the log. Each row's mark: ✓ checked under this laptop's key, ◇ linked and accepted at the laptop, △ not vouched for yet, ✕ did not check.
  • Accept what the check found, at the laptop with the token: 🩺 Health → Accept…, tick the findings, type the token, Accept the ticked.
  • Export it at the end of each show day: Export… on Health or the Audit log tab downloads every row, signed, to check on another computer. It holds people's names and what they did: give it only to whoever checks it. From another device it works only over HTTPS.
  • Its signing key is on Health (📋 Copy): copy it into the show's paperwork, and compare it at each start.
  • Split it (the admin's alone): Split the audit log…, choose the point, type the word split. The rows before it move into a signed archive; nothing is deleted. Archiving an event can split it too.
